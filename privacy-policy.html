Privacy Policy — GeoTherm ORC Studio

Last updated: July 2026

GeoTherm ORC Studio stores all plant inputs, calculation results, performance history, and maintenance records locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your plant data.

Optional local notifications (maintenance reminders and weekly operational digest) are scheduled on-device only — no push notification server receives your data.

If you subscribe to Pro, purchase verification is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive subscription status through the store provider, not payment card details.

You may export or delete your data at any time from Settings in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address shown on the Google Play store listing for GeoTherm ORC Studio.
