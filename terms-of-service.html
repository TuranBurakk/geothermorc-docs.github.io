Terms of Use — GeoTherm ORC Studio

Last updated: August 2026

1. The service

GeoTherm ORC Studio is a mobile engineering toolkit for geothermal binary (organic Rankine cycle) plants. It provides indicative performance estimates, field calculators, and optional Pro analysis tools.

Results do not replace licensed engineering judgment, plant-specific design documents, or operational procedures. You use the app at your own risk.

2. Free features and Pro

Core plant calculation, SCADA CSV import (single file), and the 14 field tools remain available without a subscription.

Pro (in-app purchase) unlocks multi-plant profiles, bulk SCADA import, full simulation, plant benchmark, branded PDF reports, and extended history. A 14-day local trial may be offered on-device; it is not a Google Play billing trial unless one is also configured in Play Console.

3. Subscriptions and billing

Pro subscriptions are sold through Google Play (Android). Payment is charged to your store account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Manage or cancel in the app (Settings → Manage subscription) or at Google Play Subscriptions.

Refunds follow Google Play policy. We do not receive or store payment card details. Subscription status is verified through Google Play and RevenueCat.

Product IDs: geotherm_pro_monthly (every 1 month), geotherm_pro_annual (every 1 year).

4. Data

Plant inputs, results, history, and maintenance records stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
